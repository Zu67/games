# Games

## Dungeon Delver

This repository is Dungeon Delver, a terminal roguelike. It does not contain Headcount or a five-city population game. `main.py` opens with "Dungeon Delver — a terminal roguelike. Five floors, one life." The title screen says "one chance," not "one life." `game/__init__.py` is empty. `Enemy` has no defense field. `player_damage` adds the roll to attack and does not subtract anything else. `CLAUDE.md` says `ui.py` never changes player or enemy state, and that file only reads those fields. The same note lists poison, a shop room, and save files as things to add. None of those are in the game. You pick a name, then descend five floors. Floors 1 through 4 are three fights, one treasure room, and one rest, in a random order. Floor 5 is a rest and then the dragon. Floors other than 5 build the list `[COMBAT, COMBAT, TREASURE, REST, COMBAT]` and shuffle it, so the three fights are the same room type and only the order changes. Each room then picks its own line, so two fights on one floor can show the same sentence. Floor 5's rest picks one of the three rest lines. The dragon line is the only boss line, and it is taken by index rather than by a random choice. `generate_floor` uses that five-room list for every floor number other than 5. The game only asks for 1 through 5. Combat rooms use one of four lines: "Torchlight flickers across crude weapon racks lining the walls.", "The stench of blood and decay hits you as you enter.", "Bones crunch beneath your boots in this grim chamber.", or "Crude carvings of monsters mark the damp stone walls." Treasure rooms use one of three: "A dusty chest sits enticingly in the center of the room.", "Something glints in the rubble near the far wall.", or "A skeleton slumps over a small pile of valuables." Rest rooms use one of three: "A surprisingly cozy alcove with a bedroll and a fresh torch.", "A shrine radiates warmth — your wounds begin to close.", or "Clear water trickles from a crack in the wall." The dragon's room always says "The ground trembles as you step into this vast, echoing chamber."

The name is stripped, so a blank name, including spaces only, becomes Hero. You start at 30 HP, attack 8, defense 3, level 1, 0 gold, and 2 potions. The next level costs 20 XP. The HP bar is 20 cells and the XP bar is 15. The fill is `int(current / maximum * width)`, so a fraction of a cell is dropped. The color is green when the ratio is above one half, yellow when it is above one quarter, and red at one quarter and below. A maximum of 0 draws an empty red bar. At 30 HP, 16 through 30 are green, 8 through 15 are yellow, and 0 through 7 are red. 15 HP and 16 HP both fill 10 cells. 1 HP fills none, the same as 0. At 20 XP, 11 through 20 are green, 6 through 10 are yellow, and 0 through 5 are red. 1 XP fills none.

Your hit is attack plus a roll from -2 to +3, and it never falls below 1. At attack 8 and defense 3, that hit is 6 through 11. A goblin hits for 1 through 6, a skeleton for 2 through 7, an orc for 5 through 10, a cave troll for 8 through 13, and the dragon for 13 through 18. A 12 HP goblin survives one of those hits and dies on the second, including two hits of 6. An enemy hit is that enemy's attack plus the same roll, then defense is subtracted once, and it also never falls below 1. That floor is inside `enemy_damage`. The fight does not call `take_damage`. `main.py` subtracts the returned number from `player.hp` with `max(0, player.hp - dmg)`. `take_damage` has the same floor, and only the tests call it. With defense 3, `take_damage(4)`, `take_damage(3)`, `take_damage(0)`, and `take_damage(-10)` each return 1 and leave 29 HP. `take_damage(5)` returns 2 and leaves 28 HP. A potion heals 15 HP, up to your maximum. At full HP the potion is still used, the heal is 0, and the message says you have no potions left. A rest heals 15 HP and does not use a potion. Treasure is a potion 40% of the time. Otherwise it is gold equal to a roll from 5 to 20, multiplied by the floor. Winning a fight adds that enemy's XP and gold. A fight calls `gain_xp` once, so it can raise your level only once. Leftover XP stays, and the next level costs the old requirement times 1.6, rounded down. That leftover can already meet the new requirement. A dragon's 50 XP added to 19 XP leaves 49 toward a requirement of 32, and the next `gain_xp` call levels you again, including a call that adds 0. A level-up adds 10 max HP, refills HP, and adds 3 attack and 1 defense. Reaching level 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 takes 20, 52, 103, 184, and 313 XP in total. The requirement after each of those is 32, 51, 81, 129, and 206. Three won combats on each of floors 1 through 4, plus the dragon, and no flee, award 173 XP at the minimum enemy on every floor and 275 at the maximum. 173 XP reaches level 4 with 70 XP toward the next 81. 275 XP reaches level 5 with 91 XP toward the next 129. The same clear awards 87 gold at every enemy minimum and 245 at every enemy maximum. The four treasure rooms are on floors 1 through 4. Each is a potion 40% of the time, so those rooms can add no gold. When every one pays, the added gold is 50 through 200, and the combined maximum is 445.

The Cave Troll's description says "wounds close before your eyes". Its HP does not regenerate. The five lines are "A sneaky little goblin with a rusty blade", "Bones clatter as it lurches toward you", "A brutish orc with a massive axe", "A massive troll — wounds close before your eyes", and "The dungeon's ultimate guardian. It breathes fire." An unknown floor number would draw a Goblin. The game only asks for floors 1 through 5.

A hit logs "⚔️  You strike {name} for {dmg} damage!". A potion that heals logs "🧪 You drink a potion and recover {healed} HP!". A potion that returns 0 logs "You have no potions left!". A successful flee logs "💨 You slip back through the doorway!" and returns `"flee"` before the enemy hits. `run_combat` is documented to return `"win"`, `"lose"`, or `"flee"`. `handle_room` checks only `"lose"`. A win and a flee both continue to the next room. The line after the fight loop, `return "win" if player.is_alive() else "lose"`, is not reached. A kill returns `"win"` inside the loop, a death returns `"lose"` inside the loop, and the loop itself continues only while both are alive. A failed flee logs "Your escape route is cut off!", and the enemy still hits. That hit logs "💥 {name} strikes you for {dmg} damage!". If the player's hit drops the enemy to 0, the enemy does not strike in that round. A round that leaves both alive waits on "Press Enter to continue...". A death or a kill does not. That sentence is the round prompt. A kill then opens the defeat panel, which waits on "Press Enter to continue..." on its own. `show_game_over` and `show_victory` call `console.clear()` and do not wait, so the process ends on that screen. Treasure waits on "You cautiously approach... Press Enter." and the panel then waits again on "Press Enter to continue...". Rest waits on "You take a moment to rest... Press Enter." and the panel then waits again on the same continue line. Ctrl-C prints "Farewell, adventurer." and exits 0. Fleeing a fight, including the dragon, does not end the run. The next room still happens. Leaving floor 5, even by fleeing the dragon, is the win. The title banner is "⚔️   D U N G E O N   D E L V E R   ⚔️", with a space between each letter and three spaces between the two words. The line under it still says "A roguelike adventure — five floors, one chance." The stats panel rows are "Hero", "HP", "XP", "ATK / DEF", "Gold", and "Potions". The hero row is "{name}  Lv.{level}", with two spaces before Lv. Gold is "💰 {gold}" and potions are "🧪 {count}". The HP bar is 20 blocks and the XP bar is 15, using the same color rule. When leftover XP is already past the next requirement, the filled count is `int(xp / xp_to_next * width)` and can pass the width. At 49 XP toward 32, that is 22 blocks and no empty blocks. Floors 1 through 4 are headed "FLOOR  n  OF  5", with two spaces around the number and around OF. Floor 5 is headed "THE DRAGON'S LAIR". After floors 1 through 4 the game also says "Floor n cleared! The stairs descend into deeper darkness..." and waits on "Press Enter to descend..." again. Floor 5 does not show that stairs panel. The name prompt says "Enter your hero's name: ". Rich adds the colon and the space. Every floor, including the first and the dragon's lair, then waits on "Press Enter to descend...". The stats panel is titled "Stats" and its first row is labeled "Hero" even when the name is not Hero. A room panel is titled with the room type in uppercase: COMBAT, TREASURE, REST, or BOSS. The HP bar is 20 characters. It is green above half, yellow above a quarter and at exactly half, and red at a quarter or below. The filled length is the ratio times the width, rounded down. At the starting 30 HP the bar is 20 green blocks. 16 HP is green and 10 blocks. 15 HP is yellow and also 10 blocks. 8 HP is yellow and 5 blocks. 7 HP is red and 4 blocks. 0 HP is red and empty. The XP bar is 15 characters and uses the same colors. The starting 0 of 20 XP is 15 empty blocks and red. 10 of 20 XP is yellow and 7 blocks. 11 of 20 XP is green and 8 blocks. Ctrl+C prints "Farewell, adventurer." and exits 0. Ctrl+D raises `EOFError`, which is not caught, so that farewell line is not printed. `Player.take_damage` subtracts defense again and is not called by the fight. The fight subtracts defense once, inside `enemy_damage`, then reduces HP by that amount.

In a fight the prompt's choices are lowercase `a`, `p`, and `f`. The screen prints [A]ttack, [P]otion, and [F]lee. The potion label includes the count. At 0 it is dimmed and still accepted. Any other letter is rejected and the prompt repeats. Pressing Enter attacks, because the default is `a`. The prompt line is `Choose (a): `. `show_choices` is off, so `a`, `p`, and `f` are not repeated on that line. `requirements.txt` asks for `rich>=13`. Rich 13.0.0 and 15.0.0, the release that requirement installs now, both treat only a completely empty line as the default. A line of spaces is rejected with "Please select one of the available options", and the prompt is asked again. `A`, `P`, and `F` are rejected the same way, because the choice check is case-sensitive and runs before `.lower()`. Spaces around `a`, `p`, or `f` are stripped, so those still count. A flee succeeds when the draw is below 0.5, and then the enemy does not hit. A failed flee, an empty potion, and a potion drunk at full HP all still take the enemy's hit. A successful flee does not award XP or gold. At full HP a rest says "You rest, but your HP is already full." The level-up panel says "ATK +3 · DEF +1 · HP fully restored" and does not mention the +10 max HP. A potion found prints "🧪 You find a Health Potion! (+1 potion)". Gold found prints "💰 You pocket {amount} gold!". A rest that heals prints "💤 You rest and recover {healed} HP." A rest at full HP prints "💤 You rest, but your HP is already full." Defeating an enemy prints "⚔️  You defeated the {name}!" and then "+{xp} XP  ·  +{gold} gold". A level-up adds a second line, "✨ LEVEL UP! ✨  ATK +3 · DEF +1 · HP fully restored". Those three panels wait on "Press Enter to continue...". The combat prompt shows "[A]ttack  ·  [P]otion 🧪×{count}  ·  [F]lee", and at 0 potions the potion label is dimmed. The enemy panel title is the enemy name. Its body starts with the emoji and the description: Goblin 👺, Skeleton 💀, Orc Warrior 👹, Cave Troll 🧌, Ancient Dragon 🐉. Death is titled "GAME OVER" and says "YOU HAVE FALLEN". The lines are "{name} perished in the dungeon.", "Gold collected : {gold}", and "Level reached  : {level}". The win is titled "DUNGEON CLEARED". It says "VICTORY!" and "{name} has conquered the dungeon!", then "Final Level  : {level}", "Gold Earned  : {gold}", and "HP Remaining : {hp}/{max}".

The game code is `main.py` and `game/`. Tests are in `tests/`. They never import `main.py`, so they do not run a fight, a flee, or a room. `test_take_damage_subtracts_defense` expects `take_damage(8)` with defense 3 to return 5 and leave 25 HP. `test_take_damage_minimum_one` expects `take_damage(5)` with defense 100 to return 1 and leave 29 HP. `test_gain_xp_triggers_level_up` checks that `gain_xp(20)` returns true, the level is 2, and HP equals max HP. It does not check that max HP is 40, attack is 11, or defense is 4. The combat tests patch `random.randint` and check only `player_damage` and `enemy_damage`. `test_use_potion_no_potions_returns_zero` checks only that the return value is 0. It does not check HP or that the potion count stays 0. `test_gain_xp_no_level_up` checks that the level stays 1 and does not check that XP is 5. `test_is_alive` sets HP to 0 by assignment. It does not call `take_damage`. `test_heal_caps_at_max_hp` starts at 20 HP with a maximum of 30, calls `heal(20)`, and expects 10 HP healed and 30 HP remaining. No test checks that one `gain_xp` call levels only once. There are 12 tests, 8 in `tests/test_player.py` and 4 in `tests/test_combat.py`. `requirements.txt` asks for `rich>=13.0` and `pytest>=8.0`. The combat helper builds an enemy named Goblin with gold 5 and description "test". `test_player_damage_minimum_one` uses attack 1 and a roll of -2 and expects 1. `test_player_damage_uses_attack_stat` uses attack 10 and a roll of 0 and expects 10. `test_enemy_damage_reduced_by_defense` uses defense 4, enemy attack 10, and a roll of 0, and expects 6. `test_enemy_damage_minimum_one` uses defense 100, enemy attack 5, and a roll of 0, and expects 1. `test_use_potion_heals_and_consumes` starts at 10 HP, a maximum of 30, and 2 potions. It expects 15 HP healed, 25 HP left, and 1 potion left. `test_is_alive` starts at 1 HP and expects true before it assigns 0.
